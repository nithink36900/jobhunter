JOBHUNTER - HOW TO USE (no technical knowledge needed)

ONE-TIME:
  1. Install Python from https://www.python.org/downloads/
     On the first screen tick the box "Add python.exe to PATH", then click Install.

EVERY TIME:
  2. Double-click the file "Start JobHunter" (the one with the gear icon, ending in .bat).
     - The first time, a black window installs things for a few minutes. Just wait.
     - Then your web browser opens JobHunter by itself.
     - If Windows says "protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
  3. The "Setup" page opens by itself the first time. Do these in order:
       Step 1: click "Choose File" and pick your resume. It must be a Word file (.docx); other formats are not accepted.
               JobHunter reads it and fills in your name, email, phone, city, skills and job titles.
       Have more than one kind of job in mind? Add another resume with its own name, then press "Use"
       on whichever one you want JobHunter to work with.
       Step 2: paste your Apify key and click "Save key".
               (Get it at console.apify.com > Settings > API and integrations.)
       Step 3: check the details, fix anything wrong, then click "Save and find jobs".
  4. Wait for "Finished" on the Find & Apply page, then click "Jobs" to see matches.
     Click any job to see why it scored that way, interview questions and its cover letter.
  5. Back in "Find & Apply", click "Start applying". A window and a browser open for each job:
     check the form, click Submit yourself, then press Enter in the black window.

TO STOP: close the black window.

Keep this folder as it is. Your resume, details, jobs and settings are stored inside it (the "data" folder) and
never leave your computer. To use a different resume, upload it again in Setup.
Never share the file called ".env": it holds your private token.

IF THE PAGE LOOKS OLD AFTER AN UPDATE
  Old black JobHunter windows keep running the old version. Close ALL of them, then start JobHunter again.
  The Setup page shows the version at the bottom, so you can check you are on the latest one.
